How it works

  • Questions are drawn adaptively — the quiz focuses on your boundary level, not just one fixed level.
  • It builds a probability estimate across all JLPT levels (N5–N1) as you answer.
  • Early answers seed the estimate; confidence grows as more data comes in.
  • Type both the meaning and the reading. The reading field converts romaji to hiragana live as you type (like WaniKani) — or paste kana directly.
  • Knowing a kanji = giving a correct meaning + at least one reading (on'yomi or kun'yomi).
    Knowing a word = giving a correct meaning + the reading.
